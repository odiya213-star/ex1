High Speed Rail

From Seoul to Andong (KTX-Eum)

Take the bullet train KTX-Eum (KTX-이음) from Cheongnyangni Station (청량리역) or selected Seoul Station departures.

  • Duration: Only ~1 hr 55 mins to 2 hrs 10 mins!
  • Arrival: Arrives at New Andong Station (안동역), where tourist buses (210, etc.) depart right outside.
  • Booking: Book through Korail official app (KorailTalk) or foreigner booking desk at major stations.
City Bus

Bus #210 & Local Public Transit

Bus #210 (Express) is the golden route for tourists, connecting Andong Station, Downtown, and Hahoe Folk Village directly.

  • Bus 210: Andong Station ↔ Hahoe Village (approx. 45-50 min). Runs frequently.
  • Bus 567 / 67: Goes north toward Dosan Seowon academy.
  • Payment: Korean T-Money transportation card, Namane Card, or contactless T-money enabled credit cards.
Taxi Helper

How to Take a Taxi in Andong

Most local taxi drivers in Andong speak limited English. Use our built-in "Show Taxi Driver" button on any attraction card!

  • Show Screen: Tapping "Show Taxi Driver" will pop up high-contrast, huge Korean text and address.
  • Payment: Credit cards, cash, and T-Money are widely accepted in all taxis.
  • Call Taxis: Kakao T app works smoothly with international credit cards or cash option.
Crucial Info

Emergency & Foreigner Hotlines

  • Korea Travel Helpline: 1330 (Free 24/7 English, Japanese, Chinese assistance & live translation!)
  • Police: 112 | Medical Emergency: 119
  • Andong Tourist Info Center: Located right outside Andong Station exit 1. English maps and brochures available.
